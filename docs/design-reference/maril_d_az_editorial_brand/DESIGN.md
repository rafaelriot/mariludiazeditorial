## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-tier personal editorial platform that bridges literary refinement and contemporary digital publishing. Drawing direct inspiration from platforms like Substack, Notion, and premium Squarespace publications, the brand communicates intellectual authority, warmth, and intimate clarity.

### Tone & Sensibility
- **Authoritative yet Approachable:** Grounded in deep navy structure, balanced by tactile organic rose and botanical sage accents.
- **Editorial Pace:** Prioritizes deliberate reading rhythms, generous white space, curated essays, and focused long-form layouts over frantic transactional density.
- **Craft & Tactility:** Uses soft linen backgrounds, gentle ink borders, and whispered depth to emulate archival stationery translated into a digital space.

## Colors

The palette balances classical deep tones with warm, natural neutrals and soft mineral highlights.

- **Primary (`#063B73` - Azul Marino):** Anchor tone for typography, high-impact titles, navigation landmarks, primary actions, and institutional authority.
- **Secondary (`#D996A6` - Rosa Empolvado):** Subtle accent for highlights, decorative badges, pull quotes, thematic tags, and gentle interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#A8B58A` - Verde Salvia):** Organic supporting tone denoting growth, quiet reflections, success states, and calm category dividers.
- **Neutral Light (`#FBF9F7` - Lino Natural):** Warm, paper-like background that eliminates sterile stark-white glare and provides tactile reading comfort. Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) is reserved for card surfaces and elevated containers.
- **Text & Ink:** High-contrast text uses `#0B2038` and `#1C2E42` to retain navy undertones without falling into harsh black.

## Typography

The type scale combines **Merriweather** for literary character with **Inter** for clean readability across modern digital interfaces.

- **Headlines:** Merriweather delivers warm serif authority, balancing classic editorial craftsmanship with digital legibility. Display headlines feature tighter tracking and open leading to avoid ink crowding.
- **Body & Long-form:** Inter is calibrated with an expanded line height (`1.875rem` on 18px body copy) mimicking classic book setting, minimizing optical fatigue during extended reading sessions.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Inter SemiBold at reduced sizes (`0.75rem`–`0.875rem`) provides functional contrast against editorial serifs, often rendered with subtle tracking for tags, timestamps, and section indices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout architecture relies on a 12-column adaptive fluid grid engineered for long-form publishing and multi-format editorial storytelling.

- **Reading Well:** Essay, article, and manifesto views are bound to a strict single-column maximum width of `720px` centered within the canvas, preventing excessive line lengths and preserving optical immersion.
- **Curated Multi-Column Layouts:** Feature showcases and story grids occupy full 12-column expanses on desktop (`1280px` max container) with `2.5rem` gutters, reflowing to a 6-column configuration on tablet and single-column on mobile.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous vertical sections leverage `space-xl` and `space-xxl` to allow content blocks to breathe naturally, evoking luxury publication spreads.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth follows a tactile, print-inspired strategy relying primarily on subtle background tonal layering complemented by warm, diffused shadows.

- **Background & Canvas:** The root canvas rests on `#FBF9F7` (Lino Natural). Story cards, reader panes, and drawers use `#FFFFFF` to stand out with soft definition.
- **Diffused Ambient Shadows:** Avoid sterile, cold drop-shadows. Shadows are tinted with deep navy and warm gray at low opacities:
  - *Card Base Elevation:* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(6, 59, 115, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(6, 59, 115, 0.02)`.
  - *Interactive Hover:* `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(6, 59, 115, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(6, 59, 115, 0.03)`.
  - *Modal & Sticky Navigation:* `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(6, 59, 115, 0.12)`.
- **Delicate Outlines:** Pair elevation with a hairline border of `rgba(6, 59, 115, 0.08)` to maintain structural integrity across high-DPI displays.

## Shapes

The geometric identity utilizes soft, organic contours (`roundedness: 1`). 

- **Containers & Cards:** Small corner radii (`0.25rem` to `0.5rem`) lend structure without appearing rigid or clinical.
- **Buttons & Tags:** Interactive pills, chip indicators, and badges adopt full pill or soft radius variants (`0.25rem` to `9999px`) where contextual warmth is desired.
- **Imagery:** Photography framing uses smooth, soft curves (`0.5rem` to `0.75rem`) to integrate portraiture and editorial imagery seamlessly into the layout.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in deep navy (`#063B73`) with white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.25rem` radius, horizontal padding `1.5rem`, height `2.75rem`. Hover shifts slightly lighter (`#094E96`) with a smooth 150ms ease transition.
- **Secondary / Soft:** Subtle blush fill (`rgba(217, 150, 166, 0.18)`) with navy text (`#063B73`). On hover, color transitions to deeper tinted blush.
- **Ghost / Editorial Link:** Underlined typography with `1.5px` offset in `#D996A6`, featuring an animated underline expansion on hover.

### Story & Essay Cards
- **Structure:** White (`#FFFFFF`) surface set over `#FBF9F7`, bounded by a `1px` border of `rgba(6, 59, 115, 0.08)` and base ambient shadow.
- **Internal Rhythm:** Image aspect ratio 16:10 or 3:2, category tag in Inter uppercase (`label-sm`), title in Merriweather (`headline-sm`), reading time and date in muted secondary slate.
- **Microinteraction:** On hover, transforms `-2px` along the Y-axis accompanied by shadow softening.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Category:** Pill-shaped capsules with `0.25rem` padding vertical, `0.75rem` horizontal.
- **Variants:**
  - *Rose Theme:* Background `rgba(217, 150, 166, 0.15)`, text `#8E4B5B`.
  - *Sage Theme:* Background `rgba(168, 181, 138, 0.20)`, text `#4F5C38`.
  - *Navy Outline:* Transparent background, border `1px solid rgba(6, 59, 115, 0.20)`, text `#063B73`.

### Input Fields & Newsletter Capture
- **Inputs:** Crisp white background, `1px solid rgba(6, 59, 115, 0.16)`, `0.25rem` border radius, inner padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Active focus introduces an `#063B73` outline with a subtle `3px` focus ring in `rgba(6, 59, 115, 0.10)`.
- **Newsletter Banner:** Embedded full-width container in warm sage or linen with serif callout header and inline email capture field.

### Blockquotes & Pull Quotes
- Left border `3px solid #D996A6`, indented `1.5rem`, rendered in italic Merriweather (`headline-md`) with navy ink (`#063B73`), followed by a subtle attribution tag in Inter (`label-sm`).
